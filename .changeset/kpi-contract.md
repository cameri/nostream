docs: six KPIs, their base measurements and their thresholds

Three KPI define what "v4 is done" means — NIP parity as a per-NIP ledger keyed to a test
with a recorded inversion, a performance comparison against the recorded benchmark artifact,
and zero migration rounds that open a directory the pin did not hold — and three measure the
build process itself, from the job records that already exist. Two parity proxies were
measured and rejected: a stub count reads zero across 282 Rust files, and per-NIP modules
overlap the advertised set at 8 of 25, so neither can fail. No package version changes.
